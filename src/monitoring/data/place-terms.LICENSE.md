# Place terms

`place-terms.ts` is a small offline gazetteer of:

- English month and weekday names
- common holidays
- cuisine and food-type words
- New York City boroughs and neighborhoods
- civic landmarks (parks, stations, bridges, museums)

It is **not** a restaurant or bar list. Venue names from tests or reviews
must not be added here.

Sources: widely published geographic names (NYC boroughs/neighborhoods and
civic landmarks) and generic English calendar/cuisine vocabulary. No
third-party dump is vendored.
